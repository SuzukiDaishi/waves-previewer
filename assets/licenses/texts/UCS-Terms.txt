Universal Category System (UCS)

The Universal Category System is a public, community-driven category list for
sound effects libraries, published at https://universalcategorysystem.com/ .

The UCS initiative publishes the category list free of charge for everyone to
use, in both commercial and non-commercial contexts, and asks only that the
system be used as published rather than forked into incompatible variants.

NeoWaves embeds the UCS v8.2.1 category list (CatID / Category /
SubCategory / Explanations / Synonyms) as offline lookup data in
`src/metadata/data/ucs_8_2_1.tsv`. No UCS audio content is redistributed.

Source workbook: "UCS v8.2.1 Full List.xlsx"

Conner's Impulse Response Library, version 2.0.0
https://github.com/itsmusician/IR-Library

A public library of impulse responses by Conner, with contributions from
Gater (Rooms/Historical/Menorcan Spanish Civil War Bunkers) and Liam G
(Rooms/Public/University of Central Florida/Engineering II Atrium Stereo Clap).
NeoWaves carries it unmodified: the whole library as a git submodule under
vendor/IR-Library, and a selection of it (assets/ir/bundled.txt) installed in
ir\ beside NeoWaves for the Reverb. Equipment in the library has been given
generic descriptive names; the library's References.md lists, for
identification only, the gear used. None of its owners is affiliated with or
endorses the library or NeoWaves.

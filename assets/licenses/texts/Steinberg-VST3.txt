Before that release the SDK was dual-licensed GPLv3 / proprietary, and a
commercial VST 3 host had to either accept GPLv3 for its binaries or sign
Steinberg's own agreement. Neither applies any more.

NeoWaves in any case redistributes no Steinberg source. It reaches VST 3
through the `vst3` Rust crate, which supplies its own MIT OR Apache-2.0
bindings to the VST 3 C interface and ships no SDK files.

TRADEMARK
---------

The licence change covers copyright only. "VST" remains a registered trademark
of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.

    VST is a registered trademark of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.

Under the MIT licence, *using the trademark at all is optional*: nothing
obliges a project to display the VST name or the VST Compatible logo, and
declining to use them carries no obligation whatsoever. Steinberg's usage
guidelines attach only if you choose to brand with it, and then they ask for
the ® symbol on first use, the attribution line above in product credits and
documentation, and the VST Compatible logo on packaging and on every web page
promoting the product. Not following them is a trademark matter and does not
affect the MIT rights to the SDK.

NeoWaves does not brand itself with VST. Where "VST3" appears it names a plug-in
format, a file extension or the operating system's own plug-in folder -- the
descriptive use every DAW makes of it -- in three user-visible strings: the
plug-in file-picker filter and two "not a recognized VST3/CLAP plugin" errors.
The attribution above is reproduced here regardless, because it costs nothing
and is the courteous thing to do.

If the product name, icon or marketing ever adopts the VST name or logo, the
guidelines above start to apply and should be read in full:
https://steinbergmedia.github.io/vst3_dev_portal/pages/VST+3+Licensing/Usage+guidelines.html

A NOTE ON ASIO
--------------

The same round of changes moved ASIO in the opposite direction — to GPLv3 /
proprietary. NeoWaves does not use ASIO: `cpal` is built without its `asio`
feature and no ASIO crate appears in Cargo.lock. Enabling it would pull GPLv3
into the binary, which would in turn make the Fraunhofer FDK AAC encoder
undistributable (see that entry).

See https://www.steinberg.net/press/2025/vst-3-8/ and
https://github.com/steinbergmedia/vst3sdk
